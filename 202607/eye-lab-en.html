Your tape

Same settings, but no two are drawn by the same hand. Where you tear it decides which faces come home with you.

How to play
Drag the eyes to change their height and how far apart they sit.
Tap an eye to change its shape — both eyes change together.
Press and hold one eye to change only that one.

Drag anywhere on the paper and both eyes look at your finger.
Touch between the eyes to cross them. The closer you get, the more they cross.
Tap the paper outside the face to look straight ahead again.

Pick the last face on the roll for a tape of eyes on their own.
